Tern

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Tern is a mail app. It connects directly from your device to your email provider (Google Gmail or Apple iCloud Mail). The developer of Tern does not collect, store, sell or share your email or personal data.

What stays on your device

Your messages, contacts and settings are downloaded to and stored only on your device. Your Gmail sign-in token and iCloud app-specific password are kept in the device keychain. Sorting, summaries and draft suggestions are done on your device using Apple's on-device models; your mail is never sent to an AI service.

Notifications

To show new-mail alerts, Tern uses a small relay service. For Gmail, Google sends the relay a notice that your inbox changed, containing your email address and a change number only; the relay passes this to Apple's push service so your iPhone can fetch the new email itself. The relay keeps your email address and your device's push token so it knows where to send alerts. For iCloud, if you use Tern on a Mac, your Mac can pass an alert's sender and subject through the relay to your iPhone; these are delivered immediately and never stored. The relay never stores the content of your email. Removing an account in Tern stops these alerts.

Google user data

Tern's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to show, search, organise and send your email within the app, and is not transferred to anyone.

Analytics and tracking

Tern contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Deleting your data

Remove an account in Tern's Settings, or delete the app, to remove all mail and sign-in details from your device. You can also revoke Tern's Gmail access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
