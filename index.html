Tern

Email that sorts itself into what needs a reply.

Tern is a fast mail app for Gmail and iCloud. It splits your inbox into Needs reply and FYI, keeps newsletters out of the way, and lets you clear mail with a swipe.

  • Needs reply / FYI split, with newsletters in their own place
  • Snooze, reminders and send later
  • Instant alerts for new mail
  • Smart sorting and draft help that run entirely on your iPhone

Download on the App Store · Download for Mac

Tern has no accounts, no servers that store your mail, and no tracking.
