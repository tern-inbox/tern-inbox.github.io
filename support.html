Support

Need help or found a bug? Open a support request and it'll be answered as soon as possible.

Adding Gmail

Go to Settings → Add Gmail and sign in with Google. If Google says the app isn't verified, tap Advanced, then Go to Tern.

Adding iCloud Mail

Apple requires an app-specific password for mail apps other than Apple Mail. Create one at account.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords, then paste it into Settings → Add iCloud along with your iCloud email address.

Notifications

Allow notifications when asked, or turn them on in the iPhone Settings app → Tern. Gmail alerts arrive instantly; iCloud alerts arrive when iOS lets Tern check, usually within the hour.

Removing your data

Remove an account in Tern's Settings, or delete the app. See the privacy policy.
